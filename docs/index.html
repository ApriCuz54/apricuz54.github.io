Research and projects

AMD co-op, 2026. Open RFC

Kernel-owned LoRA fusion for quantized layers in vLLM

Serving many LoRA adapters on a 4-bit model normally means a separate adapter pass after every GEMM. I fold the adapter expand into the W4A16 GEMM's epilogue so it happens while the output tile is still on chip. Off by default, with a fallback to the standard path.

Up to ~1.4× faster quantized GEMMs and 25–29% more serving throughput.

First author, IEEE AIMLSys EDGE-X 2025

Independent research, Sep 2026 – present

Agent Civilizations: when instructions compete

It began as multi-agent games (pricing, the prisoner's dilemma, reputation, a shared commons) and grew into a pre-registered study across twelve models from six families: do those lessons help agents on everyday tasks like negotiating, picking which worker to trust, sharing a budget and handling refunds?

Mostly they didn't, and the report keeps those nulls in. Later phases asked what a bad actor can get away with, and what actually contains one inside a fleet of agents.

One rogue in a fleet of five: only a hard cap contains it

One misbehaving agent costs the fleet about 14% of its output. Records, warnings and identity checks barely move that; capping what any one agent can take or pass on cuts it by 77%, on every model.

Phase F, pre-registered, 6 models, two fleet tasks. Damage cap −10.8 points (95% CI −14.6 to −6.7). An exclusion vote also cut harm but expelled an honest member in 19–67% of cells.
